  <h2>What I work on</h2>
  <p>
    My work sits at the intersection of environmental governance, biodiversity policy,
    and spatial analysis. I hold a PhD Fellowship at the Institute of Geographic Sciences
    and Natural Resources Research (CAS-IGSNRR), and previously worked as a Research
    Associate at ICIMOD (International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development) in Nepal.
  </p>
  <p>
    A recurring thread across this work is a dual positionality — engaging as both a
    community member and a researcher — which shapes how I think about Indigenous
    knowledge systems and their relationship to formal science, particularly in
    international policy processes like the CBD, IPBES, and the Global Biodiversity
    Framework.
  </p>
