I am a wildlife researcher based in Beijing, currently focused on ecological modeling and landscape-level conservation at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research (IGSNRR).

My background includes tenure at ICIMOD, where I managed the HKBIF portal. I specialize in integrating Indigenous knowledge with advanced geospatial tools to protect species like the Smooth-coated Otter and the Asiatic Golden Cat.
