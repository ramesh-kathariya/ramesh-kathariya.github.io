About Me

I am an environmental science professional with advanced training in biodiversity conservation, geospatial technologies, and ecological modeling. Currently, as a Research Associate at ICIMOD, I bridge the gap between field research, community outreach, and policy engagement.

As a member of an Indigenous community, I am deeply committed to uplifting Indigenous voices in science by integrating traditional knowledge with innovative conservation practices. My research has a strong focus on species-level conservation—particularly the Smooth-coated Otter—and scaling these insights to landscape-level management.
