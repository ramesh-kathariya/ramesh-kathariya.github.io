Profile

I am an Ecologist and PhD Fellow at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research (IGSNRR), Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research interests lie at the intersection of biodiversity conservation, landscape ecology, and geospatial technologies.

Previously, I served as a Research Associate at ICIMOD, where I worked on transboundary conservation and managed biodiversity data systems. I am passionate about bridging the gap between field-level wildlife research and landscape-level ecological modeling, while ensuring the inclusion of Indigenous voices in conservation science.
