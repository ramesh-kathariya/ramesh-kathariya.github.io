About Me

I am an ecologist currently pursuing my PhD at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research (IGSNRR), Chinese Academy of Sciences in Beijing. Previously, I was a Research Associate at ICIMOD, where I focused on transboundary conservation (OECMs) and biodiversity data management.

My work integrates field-based wildlife research with advanced spatial modeling. I am a strong advocate for Indigenous-led conservation and the use of Open Data to solve complex ecological challenges in the Hindu Kush Himalaya and beyond.
