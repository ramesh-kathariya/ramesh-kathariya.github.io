<h1>About</h1>

<p>
  I am a PhD Fellow at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources
  Research (CAS-IGSNRR), Chinese Academy of Sciences, based in Beijing. Before this,
  I worked as a Research Associate at ICIMOD (International Centre for Integrated
  Mountain Development) in Nepal.
</p>

<p>
  My work spans environmental governance, biodiversity policy, GIS and spatial
  analysis, and mountain development — carried out through peer-reviewed research,
  institutional communications, and data analytics. I frequently engage with
  international policy processes, including the Convention on Biological Diversity
  (CBD), the Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem
  Services (IPBES), and the Global Biodiversity Framework (GBF).
</p>

<p>
  A thread that runs through this work is a dual positionality — I approach questions
  of Indigenous knowledge and its relationship to formal science both as a community
  member and as a researcher, and I try to keep that positionality visible rather than
  incidental in how I write and present.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>

    level of biodiversity.
  </li>

    Convening paper on human–elephant coexistence programming in the Mechi corridor,

    Development Initiative (KLCDI).
  </li>
  <li>
    "Beyond Respondents: What Climate Science Misses When Indigenous Peoples Are Not

    Systems.
  </li>
  <li>
    Comparative analysis of National Biodiversity Strategies and Action Plans (NBSAPs)
    across 41 Asia-Pacific countries, built from CBD Seventh National Reports.
  </li>
</ul>
